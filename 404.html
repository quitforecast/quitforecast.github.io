If you came here looking for help right now, do not go hunting through a website.

In the US: call 988 or text 988 for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline. 1-800-662-4357 is the SAMHSA National Helpline, free, confidential and open every day. 1-800-784-8669 is 1-800-QUIT-NOW, for quitting smoking and vaping. If someone is in immediate danger, call 911.

Outside the US, your local emergency number works the same way, and findahelpline.com lists crisis lines by country.
